I live in Kraków, Poland.

For years I worked as a DevOps engineer, mostly on cloud infrastructure. Now I run open LLMs on my own hardware and
build agents on top of them: local inference, agent harnesses, evals and benchmarks, and the documentation that makes
it all reproducible.

I believe local, open-source LLMs are the best defence against the centralisation of intelligence and power.

I plan to stay in this hyperrun of building and managing many agents at once. I'm always happy to talk to and meet
people who work on similar things, so feel free to reach out on X or LinkedIn.
